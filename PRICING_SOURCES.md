# 2026 pricing research used by the configurator

Research date: September 27, 2026.

The estimator uses ranges rather than a single price because construction pricing changes materially with location, wind exposure, access, soil, engineering, product, labor and permitting.

## Published market references

- **Angi — 2026 garage construction:** reports approximately $58/sq ft average for professional garage construction in Tampa, with a broad range depending on project complexity. It also reports $6–$12/sq ft for a garage slab and $1,200–$1,500 as a typical permit allowance. Source: https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-it-cost-build-garage/fl/tampa
- **Angi — 2026 national garage construction:** reports exterior construction ranges such as $15–$35/sq ft for metal garages, $30–$45 for brick and $5–$30 for brick veneer. Source: https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-it-cost-build-garage.htm
- **This Old House — 2026 siding:** reports installed averages around $6.13/sq ft for vinyl, $9 for fiber cement and $9.66 for stucco. Source: https://www.thisoldhouse.com/siding/siding-replacement-cost
- **HomeAdvisor — 2026 siding:** reports broad installed ranges including vinyl $3–$12, fiber cement $5–$13.50, brick $9–$28 and steel $4–$8 per sq ft. Source: https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/siding/
- **Fixr — detached garage roofing:** reports approximately $2–$5/sq ft for asphalt shingles, $5–$20 for metal and $7.50–$17 for tile, installed. Source: https://www.fixr.com/costs/build-detached-garage
- **NAHB — July 2026 material-price update:** residential building-material prices excluding energy were up 5% year over year in July 2026; softwood lumber was up 17.3% year over year. This is why the calculator uses ranges and should not be treated as a fixed bid. Source: https://www.nahb.org/blog/2026/08/july-material-prices

## How the application converts this research into a project estimate

The model estimates separately:

1. concrete slab/foundation;
2. framing, sheathing and general structure;
3. exterior cladding;
4. roof covering/assembly;
5. garage door and hardware;
6. windows;
7. trim/fascia/soffit;
8. gutters/downspouts;
9. attic/storage when selected;
10. electrical allowance when selected;
11. permitting allowance;
12. engineering/design allowance.

A modest general-conditions/mobilization factor is then applied. The displayed number is intentionally a planning range, not a contractor proposal.

## Code/compliance warning

The material catalog is a visualization and budgeting catalog. It does **not** declare a material legal for every jurisdiction. Final approval depends on the adopted building code, product approvals, wind zone, fire requirements, flood exposure, manufacturer installation instructions and the complete wall/roof assembly.
