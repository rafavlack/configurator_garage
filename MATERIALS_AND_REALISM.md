# Materials, construction assemblies and pricing basis

The configurator separates **visual material selection** from **code compliance**. A material shown in the UI is not automatically approved for every jurisdiction, wind zone, fire zone or wall/roof assembly.

## Exterior cladding — 2026 planning ranges

Installed ranges used by the estimator are planning allowances:

| Material | Configurator allowance | Published reference | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Vinyl siding | $5.50–$8.50/sq ft | This Old House: about $6.13 installed | Low-maintenance cladding |
| Fiber cement | $7.50–$12/sq ft | This Old House: about $9 installed | Heavier; installation details matter |
| Stucco | $8–$12/sq ft | This Old House: about $9.66 installed | Assembly and substrate vary |
| Brick veneer | $12–$22/sq ft | HomeAdvisor: $9–$28 installed | Veneer is not structural brick |
| Architectural metal | $5–$9/sq ft | HomeAdvisor steel: $4–$8 installed | Panel profile and substrate vary |

## Roofing — 2026 planning ranges

| Roof covering | Configurator allowance |
|---|---:|
| Architectural asphalt shingle | $2–$5/sq ft installed |
| Standing seam metal | $5–$20/sq ft installed |
| Concrete tile | $7.50–$17/sq ft installed |

Fixr reports approximately $2–$5/sq ft installed for asphalt, $5–$20 for metal and $7.50–$17 for tile for detached-garage roofing. Actual roof assemblies also include underlayment, flashing, ventilation, fasteners and code-specific requirements.

## Garage construction baseline

The estimator uses a separate structural allowance rather than hiding everything inside a single square-foot number. This includes framing, sheathing and general structural labor. The result is then cross-checked against current garage construction ranges.

For example, Angi's 2026 Tampa data reports approximately $58/sq ft average for professional garage construction, with substantial variation based on size, attachment, materials and finish level.

## Important pricing limitation

This calculator is intentionally a **planning estimator**, not a contractor bid. It does not know the lot, soil, flood zone, wind zone, utility distance, existing electrical service, drainage, demolition, HOA requirements, exact product, engineering requirements or local permit fees.

The application should therefore present a range and send the selected configuration to TGB for final pricing.
